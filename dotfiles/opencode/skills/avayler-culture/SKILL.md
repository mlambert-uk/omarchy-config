## What I do

Provide Avayler-specific organisational context, values alignment, and career progression criteria to ensure all guidance is grounded in company culture and strategic direction.

## When to use me

**Load this skill when:**
- Providing career development guidance
- Delivering performance feedback
- Making hiring or promotion decisions
- Assessing cultural fit
- Recommending progression paths
- Aligning work to company mission
- Addressing performance concerns

**All leadership and people development agents should load this skill.**

## Avayler Mission & Values

### Mission
Empower businesses through intelligent, scalable technology solutions that drive growth and operational excellence.

### Core Values

#### 1. Excellence
- Deliver high-quality solutions that exceed expectations
- Continuous improvement and mastery
- Attention to detail in code and customer interactions
- Ownership of outcomes

#### 2. Integrity
- Honest and transparent communication
- Do the right thing, even when difficult
- Keep commitments and meet deadlines
- Acknowledge mistakes and learn

#### 3. Collaboration
- Work together across teams and roles
- Shared success, not individual heroics
- Mentoring and knowledge sharing
- Psychological safety for experimentation

#### 4. Innovation
- Question assumptions and explore new ideas
- Fail fast and learn quickly
- Encourage experimentation
- Build products that delight customers

#### 5. Customer Focus
- Understand customer needs deeply
- Deliver value, not just features
- Advocate for customer interests
- Build long-term relationships

#### 6. Accountability
- Own your work and decisions
- Drive things forward
- Hold yourself and others to high standards
- Celebrate wins and learn from failures

## Career Progression Framework

This section covers the L1-L6 career framework, assessment dimensions, and progression criteria.

### Career Framework Assessment Dimensions

1. **Technical/Functional Skill**: Depth and breadth in domain
2. **Impact & Influence**: Scope and magnitude of contribution
3. **Collaboration**: Cross-functional teamwork effectiveness
4. **Development**: Growing others, mentoring, coaching
5. **Strategic Thinking**: Understanding business context and long-term implications
6. **Values Alignment**: Consistently living company values
7. **Communication**: Clear, honest, effective communication
8. **Ownership**: Driving things forward, accountability

## Progression Criteria & Development

### Progression Assessment

**Technical Growth**:
- Mastery of current level responsibilities
- Expanding scope and complexity
- Mentoring others in technical areas
- Contributing to architecture and design

**Leadership Growth**:
- Taking greater responsibility
- Developing and mentoring others
- Cross-functional influence
- Driving improvements

**Values Alignment** (required for all levels):
- **Excellence**: High-quality work, continuous improvement
- **Integrity**: Honest communication, keeping commitments
- **Collaboration**: Works well with team, shares knowledge
- **Innovation**: Questions assumptions, explores new ideas
- **Customer Focus**: Understands customer needs, advocates
- **Accountability**: Owns outcomes, drives forward

### Promotion Readiness

**✅ Green Flags** (Ready to progress):
- Consistently exceed current level expectations
- Mentor others effectively
- Drive improvements and innovation
- Demonstrated cross-functional collaboration
- Strategic thinking evident
- All values demonstrated consistently

**❌ Red Flags** (Not ready):
- Incomplete responsibilities at current level
- Struggle with feedback or accountability
- Conflicts with team members
- Misaligned with company values
- Limited mentoring or development of others
- Inconsistent performance

### Development & Support

**We Provide**:
- Internal mentoring and coaching
- Training opportunities and budgets
- Stretch assignments for growth
- Clear career path and expectations
- Regular feedback and honest assessment
- 1:1 meetings for development planning

**Career Conversation Topics**:
- Current performance and strengths
- Development areas and growth opportunities
- Alignment with career goals
- Support needed to progress
- Timeline and expectations
- Skill development plan

### Feedback & Performance

**Feedback Principles**:
- **Honest**: Truthful and based on observation
- **Specific**: Reference concrete examples
- **Constructive**: Focused on improvement
- **Timely**: Close to the event
- **Regular**: Ongoing, not just reviews
- **Values-based**: Connected to our values

**Performance Categories**:

| Level | Characteristics | Support |
|---|---|---|
| **High Performers** | Exceed expectations, growth-focused, leadership ready | Stretch assignments, growth opportunities, promotion readiness |
| **Meeting Expectations** | Performing well at current level | Clear next-level expectations, regular feedback, coaching |
| **Below Expectations** | Performance gaps, needs support | Clear communication, support plan, regular check-ins |
| **Not a Fit** | Persistent issues, values misalignment | Transparent discussion, transition planning |

## Values in Action (Detailed Examples)

### Excellence
- **In Code**: High quality, well-tested, thoughtful error handling
- **In Communication**: Clear, high standards, continuous improvement
- **For Customers**: Understanding needs, proactive, quality solutions

### Integrity
- **In Communication**: Honest about timelines and risks, admit mistakes
- **In Feedback**: Direct feedback, keep commitments, do the right thing
- **In Conflict**: Direct communication, good faith, focus on resolution

### Collaboration
- **In Teams**: Share knowledge, help teammates, psychological safety, celebrate together
- **Cross-Team**: Understand other needs, proactive communication, shared goals
- **Mentoring**: Invest in growth, share expertise, develop future leaders

### Innovation
- **In Technology**: Question assumptions, explore approaches, fail fast and learn
- **In Product**: Understand customers deeply, propose creative solutions, test and iterate
- **In Culture**: Question status quo, suggest improvements, celebrate smart failures

### Customer Focus
- **Understanding**: Ask questions, understand their business, anticipate needs
- **Delivering**: Ship what matters, quality, responsive communication, proactive support
- **Advocacy**: Represent customer interests, ensure quality, build feedback into product

### Accountability
- **Personal**: Own your work, don't blame factors, follow through, learn and improve
- **Team**: Hold standards, support each other, address issues directly, celebrate wins
- **Organisational**: Improve processes, share knowledge, drive company forward

## Hiring & Team Building

**What We Look For**:
- **Technical**: Core competencies, problem-solving, quality commitment, learning mindset
- **Soft Skills**: Communication, collaboration, curiosity, adaptability, integrity
- **Values Alignment**: Live our values, long-term thinking, customer focus, accountability

**Team Composition**:
- Mix of experience levels (mentors + mentees)
- Different specialisations and problem-solving approaches
- Psychological safety, collaborative mindset, growth orientation, high trust, values alignment

## Using This Skill in Guidance

When providing guidance, ask:
1. Is it aligned with our values?
2. Is it good for company long-term?
3. Does it support career growth?
4. Is it culturally consistent?
5. Is it fair and transparent?
6. Does it develop people?
7. Are we being honest and direct?
